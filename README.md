## The word

> Creditfarmer

The boss who harvests other people's credit and presents it as his own.
He plants nothing, tends nothing, yet shows up at harvest time with the
biggest basket. It makes a reader laugh, then nod — the most recognised
boss behaviour there is, in one fresh word.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` — one word, 12 characters,
UTF-8, inside the competition's 1–30 character gate.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
